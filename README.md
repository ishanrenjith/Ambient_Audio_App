# Ambient_Audio_App

## Full Description
Ambient Audio brings one of the most beloved AirPods features to every
Android device and headphone — automatically reducing media volume the moment
you start speaking, then restoring it once you go quiet.

Whether you're listening to music, a podcast, or a video, Ambient Audio
detects your voice in real time and ducks the volume so you can hear yourself and others
clearly — no pausing, no reaching for your phone, no missing a beat.

---

**HOW IT WORKS**

Start listening with one tap. The app monitors your microphone and automatically:

- Reduces media volume when your voice is detected
- Restores volume after a configurable period of silence
- Works with any headphones, earbuds, or your phone's speaker

---

**TWO DETECTION MODES**

- RMS Mode — Fast, lightweight amplitude detection. Minimal battery impact.
Perfect for quiet environments.
- Neural Net Mode — Uses the YAMNet AI model to classify audio in real time,
distinguishing your voice from background sounds. Ideal for noisy environments
where simple volume detection would trigger incorrectly.

---

**KEY FEATURES**

🎧 Works with any headphones — Bluetooth, wired, or none
🎙️ Automatic Bluetooth mic switching — uses your headset mic when connected
🔇 Pause or duck — choose between reducing volume or pausing playback entirely
🔊 Adjustable ducked volume — set how much to reduce (0% mute to 95%)
⏱️ Configurable silence timeout — from instant restore to 10 seconds
🎚️ Live noise meter — see background noise levels and set your threshold
📳 Background operation — keeps working when you switch to another app
⚙️ Persistent settings — all your preferences saved automatically
🔋 Battery-conscious — RMS mode has negligible battery impact

---

**PRIVACY FIRST**

All audio processing happens entirely on your device. No audio is ever recorded,
stored, or transmitted. The microphone is used only for real-time voice detection
and is never saved in any form.

---

**PERFECT FOR**

- Listening to music or podcasts while having occasional conversations
- Working from home with background audio
- Hands-free environments where pausing manually is inconvenient
- Anyone who wants the convenience of AirPods Conversation Awareness on
any device or headphone
